The Ashh Story

The Ashh Store was founded to revolutionize pop-culture apparel in India. Modeled after the iconic aesthetic and precision of The Souled Store, we bring superhero fandoms, anime legends, and high-fashion streetwear under one roof with uncompromised quality.

We engineer heavyweight drops utilizing authentic 240+ GSM combed cotton, French terry weaves, high-definition puff printing, bio-silicon softening washes, and official licenses across Marvel, Naruto Shippuden, DC Comics, and Disney.

From our design labs in Mumbai to our fulfillment centers nationwide, every seam, fabric cut, and woven label reflects world-class craftsmanship.
